📖 How to Play

Welcome to Dead by Daylight: Packlocked!
Collect every card, build your loadout, and survive using only what you've earned.

⚡ Quick Start

  1. 🩸 Open Token Rewards and award yourself Blood Tokens after each match.
  2. 📦 Spend Blood Tokens to open packs and unlock new cards.
  3. 🎒 Equip your cards to build a loadout.
  4. 🎮 Play a Dead by Daylight match using only your equipped cards.
  5. 🚪 Click Escaped or Sacrificed when the match ends.
  6. 🛒 Check the Token Shop daily and complete your Collection!

🩸 Earn Blood Tokens

Click Token Rewards to view every way to earn or lose Blood Tokens during your matches.

As you complete objectives, simply click the matching reward to keep track of your progress.

📦 Open Packs

Spend Blood Tokens to unlock new cards.

  • Basic – Mostly Common & Rare cards.
  • Entity – Better Epic chances.
  • Iridescent – Highest Legendary chance.

🎒 Inventory

Every unlocked card appears here.

  • Equip cards.
  • Sell duplicates.
  • Foil cards sell for much more.

⚙ Build Your Loadout

Before each match, equip:

  • 4 Perks
  • 1 Item
  • 2 Add-ons

🚪 Finish Your Match

Escaped returns every equipped card.

Sacrificed permanently destroys everything equipped.

🛒 Token Shop

Every 24 hours the shop refreshes with three Epic or Legendary cards.

Purchased cards become Sold Out until the next refresh.

📚 Collection

Click the Collection counter above your Inventory to see every Perk, Item, and Add-on you've discovered.

🏆 Your Goal

Collect every card while surviving with only the equipment you've earned.
