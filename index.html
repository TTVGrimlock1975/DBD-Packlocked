Getting Started

How to Play

Welcome to Dead by Daylight: Packlocked!
Collect every card, build your loadout, and survive using only what you've earned.

Quick Start

  1. Open Award Tokens and bank what you earned last match.
  2. Spend Blood Tokens on packs to unlock new cards.
  3. Equip cards to build your loadout.
  4. Play a Dead by Daylight match using only what you equipped.
  5. Record the result: Escaped or Sacrificed.
  6. Check the Token Shop and grow your Collection.

IBefore the Match

Open Packs

Spend Blood Tokens to unlock new cards.

  • Basic – mostly Common & Rare.
  • Entity – better Epic chances.
  • Iridescent – highest Legendary chance.

Inventory

Every card you have unlocked lives here.

  • Equip cards to your loadout.
  • Sell duplicates for Blood Tokens.
  • Foil cards sell for much more.

Build Your Loadout

Equip before each match:

  • 4 Perks
  • 1 Item
  • 2 Add-ons

IIDuring the Match

Earn Blood Tokens

Open Award Tokens to see every way to gain or lose Blood Tokens during a match.

Click each objective as you complete it and your total updates as you go.

IIIAfter the Match

Escaped

Every equipped card returns to your Inventory.

Sacrificed

Everything you equipped is destroyed for good.

Always Available

Token Shop

Three Epic or Legendary cards, refreshed every hour.

Buy one and it reads Sold Out until the next rotation.

Collection

Click the Collection counter above your Inventory to see every Perk, Item and Add-on you have discovered.
